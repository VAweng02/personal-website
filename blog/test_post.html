Test Post: Blog Page + Auto Table of Contents

January 22, 2026 • 4 min read

This is a test article to validate the blog layout and auto-generated table of contents. Scroll down and click the ToC items on the right to jump between sections.

Why you want blog posts on your own website

Redirecting to Medium creates friction, breaks your user journey, and weakens conversions. Hosting the articles directly on your site makes your content feel native and improves SEO.

Conversion and funnel benefits

If you want to eventually sell a product, course, or mentorship, the article should live where your CTAs and funnels live.

SEO and long-term compounding

If your website is the canonical source of content, search engines will credit your domain instead of Medium.

How the Table of Contents works

The ToC on the right is generated by scanning headings (h2/h3) in this article and rendering clickable anchor links.

Stable section links

Each section heading gets an automatic id. Clicking ToC items jumps to that heading.

Active highlight as you scroll

When you scroll, the script uses IntersectionObserver to highlight the currently visible section.

Scalability: adding new posts should be easy

The scalable pattern is: one reusable layout + separate content files. You should not need to touch your CSS or JS every time you publish.

Recommended workflow

  1. Copy the template
  2. Rename it (e.g. my-post.html)
  3. Write content using h2 / h3 headings
  4. Publish

Next steps

If this looks good, next we can build:

  • A blog homepage (blog.html) showing all posts
  • Optional: a JSON-driven blog index so you never manually edit HTML for the list

End of test post ✅
